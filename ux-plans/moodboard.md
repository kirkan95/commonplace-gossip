# Moodboard — Normal Gossip Special Edition

## Source Research
- **Logo**: Illustrated by Tara Jacoby — three people playing telephone, vibrant block colors, organic rounded edges
- **Tone**: "Sweet conspiratorial" — warm, inclusive, light-hearted drama about people you'll never meet
- **Official tagline**: "Juicy, strange, funny, and utterly banal gossip about people you'll never know and never meet"
- **Produced by**: Defector Media + Radiotopia

## Color Palette Decisions
| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Gossip Red | #D4552A | Primary accent, CTA, player controls |
| Tea Yellow | #F5C842 | Secondary accent, episode card borders |
| Blush Pink | #F0A8A0 | Soft highlights, hover states |
| Parchment | #FBF3E8 | Page background |
| Warm Sand | #E8D4BC | Card backgrounds, dividers |
| Deep Brown | #1C1008 | Body text |

Palette rationale: warm, analog, slightly vintage — evokes passing notes and whispered secrets rather than a slick tech product.

## Typography Direction
- **Display**: Bold serif (Georgia or similar) for the wordmark/title — evokes the rounded bubble font from NG's logo without requiring a custom font
- **Body**: Warm serif for descriptions and episode copy
- **Meta**: Monospace for episode numbers, durations, timestamps — creates contrast and a "data" feel

## Tagline Options for Special Edition
- **Option A** (Personal): "A very special edition — submitted by the people who love you most."
- **Option B** (In-character): "We've been hearing things. And we think you deserve to know."
- **Option C** (Conspiratorial): "These stories are true. The names have been changed. The gossip has not."

## Episode Title Style
Normal Gossip titles are vague, relational, and slightly dramatic. They describe a situation without giving it away. Examples:
- "The Incident at the Bachelorette Party"
- "The Coworker's Sister's Ex"
- "The Group Chat"
- "Something You Said at Brunch in 2019"

## UI Direction
- **Welcome screen**: Centered, minimal, parchment background — feels like receiving a secret invitation
- **Main page**: Stacked episode cards, warm background, inline audio player per episode
- **Player**: No full-screen takeover, progress bar in Gossip Red, clean controls
- **Overall feel**: Private RSS feed, not a tech product — intimate and handmade
